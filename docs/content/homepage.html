A business OS for owner-led retail.

Athena

Live in production

The owner should not have to reconstruct the business before running it. Athena keeps sales, stock, cash, services, and staff work on one operating record — and runs the operating day at Wigclub , a retail and service business in Ghana, in production today.

Read the story

  1. One connected operating record

    In-person and online sales share context with inventory, cash, services, and staff work — the day’s evidence lives in one place, not across disconnected tools.

  2. Built for real operating conditions

    The local-first register records the work before it reports success, keeps checkout independent of a cloud round trip, and reconciles with the cloud without rewriting history.

  3. Engineered for humans and AI agents

    I build Athena with AI agents inside a repository that audits its own delivery: generated maps orient contributors, evidence follows every change, and each failure that escapes becomes a new sensor. The quality bar stays human.
