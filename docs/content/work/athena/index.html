The operating problem

The owner should not have to reconstruct the business before running it.

In an owner-led retail business, the evidence of the day often lives in different places: a register total, online orders, stock notes, physical receipts, staff memory, and a list of work still waiting for someone. Before the next decision can happen, the owner has to piece those fragments back together.

That reconstruction is more than administrative friction. It makes simple questions harder to answer: What sold? What changed in stock? Which cash or service issue is unresolved? What needs attention before the day can close?

The product model

One operating record, shaped around the day.

Athena connects in-person and online sales with inventory, procurement, cash controls, services, and staff activity. The value is not that every domain has been collapsed into one screen. It is that the records can retain enough shared context for the owner to follow the work from action to evidence.

Boundary: Athena is not yet a complete cross-domain command center. Several operating surfaces are connected by shared records and workflows, while the unified exception view is still being strengthened.

Connected operations

Start with today. Keep the history close.

Store Pulse gives the operator a view of completed point-of-sale activity, including sales, transactions, items sold, comparison windows, and the products moving within the selected period. As Athena records more operating days, that history becomes available for comparison without pretending to reconstruct the paper record from before adoption.

Beyond this point-of-sale view, Athena’s reporting model can recognize completed in-person activity and fulfilled storefront activity while preserving which channel each record came from. That creates a shared commerce history without claiming every channel already appears inside Store Pulse itself.

Resilient operations

The register records the work before it reports success.

A provisioned, locally authorized register writes each cashier event to its local record before returning success, whether or not the browser appears online. That keeps connected and disconnected selling on the same command path instead of treating offline work as a weaker fallback.

  1. 01
    Check local authority

    Terminal integrity, staff identity, drawer authority, and a durable local destination still matter.

  2. 02
    Append local evidence

    The register event exists before the cashier sees success.

  3. 03
    Continue the operation

    The local read model keeps the active register usable through unreliable connectivity.

  4. 04
    Synchronize and reconcile

    Ordered, idempotent upload maps local records to cloud records and surfaces conflicts for review.

The boundary is deliberately narrow: point of sale is Athena’s offline-first workflow, and Convex becomes the cloud source of truth after accepted projection. If last-known stock or permission evidence has drifted, Athena preserves the completed local sale and creates review work rather than rewriting the receipt or hiding the history.

Engineering method

AI-assisted development needs a codebase that can explain itself.

I build Athena with AI agents, but not through prompt-in, patch-out work. The repository is designed to help contributors understand the system, produce evidence for changes, and leave the next person or agent with more context than they started with.

01

Understand the system

Give every contributor reliable context before changing anything.

  • OrientRepository policy, generated indexes, and a knowledge graph make the current shape discoverable.
02

Make change evidence

Connect each touched surface to proof that it still behaves as intended.

  • ValidateFocused tests, architecture checks, audits, types, and builds meet the changed surface.
  • ExerciseRepresentative runtime scenarios show whether key paths still boot and behave.
03

Preserve judgment

Keep delivery accountable now and useful to the next contributor.

  • ReviewDiff-aware sensors and fail-closed freshness checks stop incomplete evidence from moving silently.
  • PreserveSolution notes retain the reasoning behind substantial changes.

These safeguards make the repository more legible and reviewable for humans and agents; they do not create autonomous development, exhaustive runtime coverage, full test coverage, or guaranteed correctness. Human review and bounded authority remain part of the system.

Current state

Real operating depth, with the limits left visible.

Athena is in active development. The useful distinction is not finished versus unfinished, but what the product can support now, what still needs validation, and what I am not claiming yet.

Works

Core retail loops

Point of sale, storefront checkout, orders, inventory, procurement, cash controls, Daily Close, and staff workflows are represented across the operator and customer systems.

Being validated

The clearest owner story

The entry promise and the way operating evidence should converge are still being tested. The cross-domain exception view and proactive guidance need more work.

Not claimed

Outcomes the evidence cannot support

No revenue lift, time savings, adoption, growth, autonomous replenishment, demand forecast, or complete financial cockpit is claimed here.
