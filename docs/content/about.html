  • Pricing and billing infrastructure

    Became a go-to engineer for payment flows, led 3D Secure support for regulated markets, and built fraud prevention that reduced fraudulent payments by 65% in three months.

The work spanned lifecycle-aware guidance, backend contracts, responsive product experiences, telemetry, experimentation, and feedback capture.

Across those chapters, I progressed from delivering individual product experiences to becoming a domain expert and leading business-critical systems across frontend, backend, and infrastructure.
