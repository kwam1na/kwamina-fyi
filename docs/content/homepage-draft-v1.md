## How I Work

### I Build Around Operational Trust

The best software does not only complete the happy path.
It helps people understand what happened, recover from what went wrong, and trust the record afterward.
That shows up in Athena's daily operations model, Eventbrite payments work, and the way I think about dashboards as operating surfaces rather than static views.

### I Make Systems Observable

If a product matters, teams need to see how it behaves.
I care about analytics, monitoring, feedback loops, runtime evidence, and the instrumentation that turns production from a mystery into a learning surface.

### I Treat Rollout And Rollback As Product Work

Shipping safely is part of the user experience.
Feature flags, staged rollout, rollback plans, synthetic checks, and runbooks are not ceremony; they are how a team preserves trust while changing important systems.

### I Automate Through Evidence And Authority Boundaries

Automation should not erase accountability.
The systems I trust most act from clear records, preserve who did what, and make the boundary between recommendation, action, approval, and recovery visible.

### I Learn Domains Deeply Enough To Improve Them

I do my best work when I understand the work behind the interface.
Whether the domain is payments, creator analytics, inventory, or daily store operations, I try to learn enough to improve the shape of the system instead of only implementing the next ticket.
