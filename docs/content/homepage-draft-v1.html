Athena

Athena connects sales, stock, procurement, cash, services, and staff activity so owners can understand what happened and decide what needs attention next.

One connected operating record

In-person and online sales share context with inventory, cash, services, and staff work instead of leaving the owner to reconstruct the day across disconnected tools.

Built for real operating conditions
